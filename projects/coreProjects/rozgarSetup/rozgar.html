ABOUT THE PROJECT

Helping People Become Self-Reliant.

Rozgar Setup focuses on helping deserving individuals establish a sustainable way of earning. Rather than providing only temporary assistance, the project aims to create practical opportunities that can continue supporting an individual and their family.

With community support, the right opportunity can become a new beginning — helping people move towards greater financial independence and stability.

Sustainable Income

Supporting practical ways to earn a regular income.

Dignity & Independence

Helping people become more self-reliant.

Family Support

Stronger earning capacity can support entire families.
