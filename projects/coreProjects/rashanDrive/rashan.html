ABOUT THIS PROJECT

A helping hand can make a big difference.

Rashan Drive is focused on providing essential food support to families who are facing difficulty meeting their everyday needs. The initiative brings together community support and practical assistance so that help reaches people with dignity and care.

Every contribution can become part of a food package that supports a household and turns a difficult day into a more hopeful one.
